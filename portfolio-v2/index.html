Featured case study

Weather & Well-being

At The Weather Company, I led mixed-methods research to understand how weather shapes physical and emotional well-being for a younger, digital-native audience. The work connected consumer needs, product strategy, and marketing direction around a shared opportunity.

  • Combined app-intercept survey data with thematic analysis to identify high-value health and well-being needs.
  • Helped shape app redesign priorities, new content modules, and alert opportunities aligned with user value.
  • Created evidence teams could reuse in product, editorial, and campaign planning.

Outcome highlights

Insights informed app re-architecture, health-focused content opportunities, and research-based user stories for Product and Editorial teams.
